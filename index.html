What the book is about

Your brain, your team, and your organization, seen as a technology stack

Just like a computer system is organized in layers — processor, memory, local network, external network — this book organizes engineering leadership into four layers that depend on each other. Each layer has its own typical "bugs," and fixing a problem at the wrong layer rarely works. The book gives you a clear map of the parts of the job that never came with documentation.

CPU

Your processor

Task management, focus and priorities: how your "processor" executes day-to-day work.

RAM

Your memory

Working memory, learning, and mental load: using your temporary storage for what actually matters.

LAN

Your team

Roles, career paths and team dynamics: from a group of people to a high-performing team.

WAN

Your organization

Stakeholders, cross-team collaboration, and managing upward: connecting your team to the rest of the world.
